# post-quantum-cryptography-capstone
Cybersecurity capstone analyzing post-quantum cryptography, quantum computing threats, cryptographic protocols, and PQC migration strategies across banking, healthcare, and higher education.
# Integrating Post-Quantum Cryptography: A New Frontier in Cyber Defense and Threats

**Senior Capstone Project | Grambling State University**

**Authors:** Jocelyn Bailey, Jhada Carmel, and Jonathan Smith

## Overview

This capstone examines the growing cybersecurity risks created by quantum computing and the role of post-quantum cryptography (PQC) in protecting digital systems.

The project explores how quantum algorithms, particularly Shor's algorithm, could threaten widely used public-key cryptographic methods such as RSA and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC). It also evaluates the benefits, limitations, and implementation challenges of PQC across banking, healthcare, and higher education. Capstone Final.pdf

## Project Objectives

- Examine how quantum computing could affect current cryptographic systems.
- Explain the purpose and development of post-quantum cryptography.
- Analyze traditional and quantum-resistant cryptographic protocols.
- Review real-world cybersecurity incidents across multiple industries.
- Evaluate how PQC may strengthen long-term data protection.
- Identify challenges associated with migrating to quantum-resistant systems.
- Recommend strategies organizations can use to prepare for the post-quantum era.

## Case Studies

### Banking: JPMorgan Chase

The banking case study examines the 2014 JPMorgan Chase data breach and the security weaknesses associated with phishing, stolen credentials, server misconfiguration, and inadequate authentication controls. Capstone Final.pdf

The project also discusses security measures such as:

- End-to-end encryption
- Updated TLS configurations
- Firewalls
- Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
- Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

These controls are discussed alongside the long-term need for quantum-resistant cryptographic protections. Capstone Final.pdf

### Healthcare: Universal Health Services

The healthcare case study reviews the ransomware attack affecting Universal Health Services and the operational consequences of a major cyber incident in a healthcare environment.

The attack disrupted hospital systems, forced staff to rely on manual processes, and created significant financial and operational impact. Capstone Final.pdf

The project considers how lattice-based post-quantum cryptography could support:

- Long-term protection of healthcare records
- Secure communications
- Stronger digital signatures
- Protection against future decryption attempts

### Higher Education: University of California System

The education case study examines the University of California system data breach connected to the Accellion compromise.

The research discusses risks involving:

- Third-party vulnerabilities
- Zero-day exploits
- Credential stuffing
- Long-term exposure of sensitive academic and personal records

The project evaluates PQC as a potential long-term strategy for protecting cloud backups, academic records, and research data. Capstone Final.pdf

## Protocol Analysis

### RSA

RSA is a widely used public-key cryptographic algorithm. The project explains how its security depends on the difficulty of factoring large numbers and why sufficiently powerful quantum computers using Shor's algorithm could threaten it. Capstone Final.pdf

### TLS/SSL

Transport Layer Security (TLS) protects data transmitted across networks by combining public-key and symmetric encryption.

The project discusses the importance of proper configuration and the future need for quantum-resistant cryptographic methods within secure communications. Capstone Final.pdf

### Lattice-Based Cryptography

Lattice-based cryptography is presented as one of the leading approaches to post-quantum security.

The research discusses its resistance to known quantum attacks while also recognizing challenges such as:

- Larger key sizes
- Greater bandwidth requirements
- Increased storage demands
- Implementation complexity
- Potential future vulnerabilities

Capstone Final.pdf

## Key Findings

The project found that post-quantum cryptography offers significant potential for protecting sensitive information against future quantum-enabled attacks.

However, migration to PQC introduces technical and organizational challenges, including:

- Larger keys and signatures
- Increased bandwidth requirements
- Greater computational costs
- New implementation risks
- Side-channel vulnerabilities
- Transitional security gaps
- Limited large-scale real-world testing

Because of these challenges, organizations should approach PQC adoption gradually and strategically. Capstone Final.pdf

## Recommendations

The capstone proposes three primary strategies for preparing for the post-quantum era:

### 1. Sector-Specific Action Plans

Organizations should develop PQC adoption roadmaps tailored to their infrastructure and security requirements.

### 2. Hybrid Cryptographic Migration Strategies

Organizations should consider phased migration approaches that combine classical and quantum-resistant cryptographic systems.

### 3. Readiness Assessments and Training

Organizations should regularly evaluate their cryptographic posture, identify security gaps, and train IT personnel on PQC principles and quantum-related threats. Capstone Final.pdf
